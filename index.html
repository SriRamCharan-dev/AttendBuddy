Interactive Reference

Control it all with simple chat commands.

No complex layouts. Just type commands in Telegram to query and manage everything.

Student Commands

/stats

Get your cumulative percentage, progress meters, monthly forecasts, and bunks left.

/history

Review your logged status (present, absent, holiday) for the last 30 days.

/correct

Correct today's attendance status if you tapped incorrectly or missed the check-in.

/holidays

List all public and special holidays configured for the current month.

Admin Commands

/settings

Show settings. Change min threshold (e.g. min 75) or working days (e.g. workdays 1,2,3,4,5,6).

/holiday

View calendar. Mark today/custom dates as a holiday, or remove added holidays.

/broadcast [msg]

Broadcast a general text notification to all registered students instantly.

/monthlystats

Generate monthly report. Ranks students by attendance and displays option to broadcast the overview.
